Company Registry (bot-detection demo)

A miniature version of the kind of site the detector is meant to protect: a registry where each company has a page and a downloadable document. Browse it like a person would — every page load, link click, and download is real, and the risk panel at the bottom reflects what the detector sees.

How to use this demo. Open the browser console once to confirm the scripts load, then just navigate. The bottom panel tracks your behavior across pages (its request log lives in localStorage, so it persists as you move around). Press Evaluate risk on any page to score the combined behavioral + request-pattern feature vector against the running backend.

The normal entry point. Lists companies, each linking to its own page.
Viewing a company page and then downloading its document is the natural "browsing trail" a real researcher leaves — the detector rewards it.
Two special pages exist to demonstrate the scraping signals directly:
  • Scenario Lab — simulates what an automated script does (many requests, no navigation, sequential IDs). This is the one thing that genuinely can't be a "real" page, because a human physically can't navigate that way.
  • A honeypot page that isn't linked anywhere in the real UI — you'll only find it by enumerating URLs, which is exactly the point.
