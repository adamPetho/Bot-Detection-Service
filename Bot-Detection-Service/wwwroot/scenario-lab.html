Scenario Lab

Why this one is a control panel, not "real" browsing. Every other page in this demo produces its signals through genuine navigation and clicks. The behaviors here — dozens of downloads in a fraction of a second, walking IDs in order, downloading with no page view — are exactly what a human physically cannot do by using the site. So this page injects them directly into the shared request log, letting you reproduce a scraper's footprint on demand and then hit Evaluate risk to see it.

Reset

Clears the rolling window so a scenario starts from a clean slate. (Does not reset your session id.)

Scraper-shaped scenarios

1 · Sequential enumeration sweep

Logs 25 downloads for company-2000 … company-2024, in order, no page views.

Drives up request volume and breadth with almost no repeats, walks IDs by +1 each step, and leaves no browsing trail — it should light up the single-pass-sweep, sequential-ID, and missing-trail signals at once.

2 · Downloads with no browsing trail

Logs 12 downloads for random company ids, none of them viewed first.

Even without sequential ids, downloading things you never opened is a tell — a real researcher reads the page before pulling the document.

3 · Honeypot hit

Logs a hit on the decoy resource directly.

The same signal as visiting the unlinked honeypot page, without the navigation. Near-certain on its own.

After running a scenario, press Evaluate risk in the bottom panel. For a fair contrast, hit Reset, then go browse the directory and download a couple of companies the normal way — that should score as Human.
